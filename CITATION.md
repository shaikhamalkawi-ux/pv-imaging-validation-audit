## Primary research and dataset sources
1. Sandia National Laboratories: *Photovoltaic Module Current-Voltage and Electroluminescence Image Data (PV-IV-EL)*, DOI https://doi.org/10.11578/3818365.
2. Bommes et al. (2022): *Anomaly detection in IR images of PV modules using supervised contrastive learning*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/pip.3518.
3. Pratt, Mattheus and Klein (2023): *A benchmark dataset for defect detection and classification in electroluminescence images of PV modules using semantic segmentation*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sasc.2023.200048.
4. Pratt, Lawrence E. (2024): *Deep Learning Models for Defect Detection in Electroluminescence Images of Solar PV Modules*, University of the Witwatersrand doctoral thesis, https://hdl.handle.net/10539/42163.

The software repository hosts derived analyses, not original Sandia imagery or restricted Scopus/WoS/IEEE bibliographic exports. Author metadata, reuse licensing, and final journal policy are pending.
